# Brain Tree research notes

The 14 stops are scenes in a branching story. Read the named node in `diverged_mya` before displaying its age. Read the species, region, life stage and method together before plotting any neuron count. A goldfish retina, a sea squirt larval central nervous system and an elephant brain are three different denominators.

## Verdicts on the 17 starting claims

The source IDs below resolve in `data.json`. The corresponding stop records contain the audit excerpts. Compound claims receive one overall verdict here, with separate conclusions for their parts.

`parent` and `from_stop` point to teaching scenes. A link from a mammal to the reptile scene identifies a **shared ancestral feature described there**. It does not claim descent from a turtle. The early-branch exemplars with and without neurons cannot establish by themselves what the first animal was like. The displayed dates never directly measure the age of a synapse, a brain region, a behavior or a land transition.

## Dates, counts and differences between sources

- Most early dates use the **composite interval** in table 1 of dos Reis and colleagues. It combines uncertainty across model settings. The numeric display coordinate is the midpoint when `value_basis` says so. It is not a separately measured best date. For example, their crown vertebrate interval spans 459.3–533.8 million years. Lamanna and colleagues give about 515–645 million years as background for ancestral vertebrate brain divisions. These estimates concern related but differently framed events. Keep the model, node and bounds together. [earlydate, cer3]
- The cephalopod date uses the crown cephalopod estimate in López-Córdova and colleagues, table 1. The bird date uses Stiller and colleagues’ earliest split within Neoaves. Neither date identifies when an octopus arm or a parrot song circuit appeared. The primate range is a rounded literature range, unlike the posterior intervals in the clock tables. [cephD, birdD, primdate]
- **Octopus.** Young gives about 300 million neurons in common octopus arm ganglia. Sivitilli and colleagues summarize about 350 million in the periphery. Both place most neurons outside the central brain. Differences in rounding and the anatomical labels prevent a precise crosswalk. [young, octAlt]
- **Sea squirt.** The 177 neurons are larval **central** neurons in Ryan and colleagues’ reconstruction. Kourakis and colleagues distinguish Pacific *Ciona robusta*, formerly *C. intestinalis* type A, from *C. intestinalis* type B. Preserve names as published and label which experiment each belongs to. [squirt, squirtA]
- **Insects.** The 139,255 figure is a reconstructed female fly brain. The honeybee figure is an estimate for one class of cells in one mushroom body. Sampling an input junction in the bee and tracing a whole fly brain answer different wiring questions. [fly, beeSub, beeEM]
- **Macaque.** The 2007 paper calls the animals *Macaca mulatta* in Methods, but labels the macaque *M. fascicularis* in a figure caption. The follow-up comparison identifies the 87.35 g specimen-group mean with *M. mulatta*. The count here follows Methods and the follow-up. Olfactory bulbs were excluded and the sampled hemisphere was doubled. [macaque, extraMa]
- **Opossum.** Seelke and colleagues give 0.80 million neurons for the intact neocortical hemisphere and 0.88 million when summing separately dissected areas of the opposite hemisphere. The first estimate is in the file. Both are regional estimates. [oposN]
- **Fish.** A review places the larval zebrafish brain on the order of 100,000 neurons. The larval source used stereological sampling. The underlying adult proliferation paper reports new cells and the fraction of **all** brain cells they represent. Inferring a precise adult neuron census from that denominator would be unjustified. Goldfish retinal ganglion cell totals vary with the size of the animal. [zebReview, zebraCount, zebCount, goldN]
- **Salamanders and crows.** Counts of recorded, sequenced or light-responsive cells are experimental sample sizes unless the authors establish a census boundary. The salamander experiment recorded selected visual neurons. The crow detection task recorded forebrain activity. Neither yields a whole-brain total. [salamove, crow]
- **Mammals.** The human result is a sample mean. The elephant estimate is from one adult. Brain region matters more here than the rounding: the elephant cerebellum and cerebral cortex give very different comparisons with humans. [human, ele]

## How to audit a record

`evidence_quote` is a short searchable excerpt. For secondary evidence and summary text, `evidence_quote_ref` is a JSON Pointer from the root to an identical excerpt elsewhere in the file. The source list contains the publication record, DOI where verified, publication year and article type. A project’s year is the publication year. Each cited paper was opened through its publication record, full text or supplement. DOI checks matched the resolved publication metadata or DOI-handle target and registration metadata to the paper title. An abstract was used where it directly supported the statement. Numeric table lookups identify their table or study population when necessary.

`new` applies to the named feature at the stated scope, such as the placental bridge or the parrot vocal shell. `elaborated` means an inherited anatomical substrate was modified, including by reduction. `convergent` requires a named comparison. Region colors group entries for display; sharing a color does not establish common ancestry. An origin of null means that the source establishes the observation but cannot place its evolutionary origin. Behavioral records distinguish lesions, stimulation and intact-animal behavior from neural activity that merely correlates with a response.

A complete reference map is complete **within its named compartment**. Projects are examples, not a registry with exhaustive negative coverage. Whole-volume imaging, a single-cell gene atlas, a diagram inferred from behavior and an experimentally identified set of synapses have different evidential reach. Only anatomically identified connectivity is used as evidence for a synaptic map. The Hydra bundle study is noted alongside the comb-jelly reconstruction as an ultrastructural study, with its narrower scope. [hydraO, comb3, zebra, olfmap]

The first scene has no parent. Its inherited ciliary machinery predates the trail, so the source-stop pointer is also null. The 1997 turtle retina paper has a verified publication record but no verified DOI in this pass. For the planarian, Takeda and colleagues counted specific neuronal types across animals of different sizes. Their discussion of a possible minimum of about 10,000 brain neurons is unsuitable as a fixed species count. [cili, reptem, flatN]

Open questions in the data are editorial questions grounded in the cited experiments or reviews. Their citations identify the observation or uncertainty motivating the question. Questions about evolutionary origins should stay distinct from questions about which circuit is active in an animal today.
