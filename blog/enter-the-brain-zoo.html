Enter the Brain Zoo

Zoo Brains is an exhibit in Boston, MA, at Zoo New England, produced by the citizen science project Eyewire of Princeton University's Seung Lab in partnership with illustration students at Massachusetts College of Art and Design.

It invites visitors to venture into the brains of animals with a poster series that highlights the uniqueness of a dozen species at Franklin Park Zoo. Check out a few of the posters below.
